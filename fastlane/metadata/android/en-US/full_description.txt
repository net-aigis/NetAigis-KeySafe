NetAigis KeySafe is an independent, GPLv3-or-later Android password manager based on KeePassDX. It opens and edits KeePass KDBX databases locally and can optionally synchronize encrypted databases with a self-hosted HTTPS coordinator.

Important:
• Databases are encrypted with credentials you control.
• HTTPS coordination sends encrypted KDBX ciphertext and coordination metadata to the server you configure.
• Review docs/PRIVACY.md and docs/KEYSAFE_THREAT_MODEL.md before using real secrets with coordination.
• There is no separate Pro unlock SKU in KeySafe.
